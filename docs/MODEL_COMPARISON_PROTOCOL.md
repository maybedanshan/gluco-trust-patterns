# Model comparison extension: protocol 2.0

Exploratory extension specified after reviewing the fixed-lambda first benchmark and before running this comparison. Not preregistered; not an untouched external test. Existing v1 predictions and joint missingness experiments are preserved unchanged.

Keep v1 eligible meals, outcome, features, outer held-out people, early-history budgets (3/5/10) and identical later test sets. No new data filtering. The outer person's entire record is excluded from global fitting and hyperparameter selection.

For each outer fold, assign its training participants to five inner folds. Sort IDs by SHA-256 of `20260916|participant`, then distribute round-robin. For each candidate ridge penalty [0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100], fit the participant-balanced model separately in each inner training fold. Feature centering/scaling is fitted inside that fold only. Compute each inner-validation person's MAE over their eligible meals and average people equally across all five folds. Select minimum MAE; an exact tie favors the larger penalty. Refit on all outer training people with the selected penalty. The weighted-MSE objective and intercept treatment match v1. Selection targets unseen-person MAE, not later-history test performance.

Compare training-person-balanced mean baseline, fixed-lambda-10 pooled reference, inner-selected pooled ridge and its personalized residual-offset extension. The offset remains residual sum / (k + 5), with shrinkage fixed at 5. Add a history-only baseline that predicts the mean outcome of the first k eligible meals; it has no global features or fitting. It is unavailable for unseen people. Do not assign a fictitious history-only or personalized advantage to that setting.

For existing-history evaluation use the same later meals after the first ten eligible meals, even for k=3/5. Preserve temporal purging and do not update on later outcomes. Report participant-macro MAE. Report paired improvements: mean baseline minus selected pooled, fixed pooled minus selected pooled, selected pooled minus personalized, and history mean minus personalized (positive favors the latter model). History-only contrasts apply only to the history setting. Use 10,000 participant-bootstrap draws, seed 20260916, 95% percentile pointwise intervals. Share draws among models in each setting; common ordered participants produce the same draws across history budgets. Intervals condition on fitted overlapping folds and omit full refitting uncertainty. No multiplicity correction or confirmatory claim.

Write inner split IDs, candidate validation scores and selected penalties for every outer fold, plus exact test IDs and local per-meal predictions. Verify original data/split hashes before use. No individual glucose, meal timestamp or meal-level target is bundled in the public summary. Source-derived results remain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

Published interpolation, assumed meal-metadata availability, selection bias and limited cohort size remain limitations. Better retrospective metrics do not establish online validity, clinical usefulness or causal nutrition effects. If personalization fails to beat the history-only baseline, report that directly. The existing missingness experiment remains conditional on the v1 model; it is not evidence about the selected model until rerun under a separate extension.
