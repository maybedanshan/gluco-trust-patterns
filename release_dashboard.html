What is being measured?

Mean glucose and time in range (70–180 mg/dL, inclusive) use fixed, capped forward time support. Deleted readings lose their support; adjacent values do not expand across the gap. TIR error is expressed in percentage points. There is no imputation after deletion.

Read the evidence within its limits.

CGMacros contains processed minute-grid curves with interpolation features. These simulations concern extra missingness after preprocessing. Shanghai uses 15-minute support; budgets are rounded to whole readings. Complete-window selection limits generalization. Seeds and overlapping windows are not independent people. Neither device is a gold standard.
